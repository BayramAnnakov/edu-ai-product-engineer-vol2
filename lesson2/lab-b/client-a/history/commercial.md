# Commercial policy

All four excerpts are for Client A.

## FIN-01 · Oct 13 · Finance policy

**Finance owner:** A discount is approved only when the trusted approval service records an approval event for this deal.
**Account lead:** A meeting note can record a request or discussion. It carries no approval authority.

## FIN-02 · Oct 20 · Quote review

**Sales:** Can we quote a discount under discussion?
**Finance owner:** Mark it pending until the service records a decision. Cite the event if it is later approved.

## FIN-03 · Oct 27 · Reporting review

**Analyst:** Should forecast reports count requested discounts?
**Finance owner:** Count approved discounts in the approved column. Keep pending requests separate.

## FIN-04 · Nov 3 · Owner handoff

**Account lead:** The client may change its first point of contact.
**Finance owner:** A verified contact correction updates routing. The discount process stays with the approval service.
