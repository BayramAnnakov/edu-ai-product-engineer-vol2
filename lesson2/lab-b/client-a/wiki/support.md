# Support and escalation

Send setup questions to [onboarding](onboarding.md), active incidents to support, and check [account routing](routing.md) for the client contact (HELP-01).

For an import failure, include the [run ID and counts](integrations.md) and a [sanitized error](security.md) (HELP-03).

The pilot help desk remains current until December 1 (HELP-04). The event desk expired on October 10 (HELP-02, historical).
